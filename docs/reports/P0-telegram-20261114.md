## 2. Coverage (audit 13.11: find_chats 47 vs recent_activity 2)

Root cause, proven by matching the audit hash (`mcp_tool_audit.input_sha256`
`a91744b28b75`, 13.11 06:34 UTC): ChatGPT called `recent_activity(since=7d,
limit_chats=200, limit_messages_per_chat=1, include_archived_dialogs=true,
include_telemetry=true)`. `scan_dialogs` passed the flag to Telethon as `archived=True`,
which means ONLY the archive folder. Reproduced live before the fix: 2 private chats.
`find_chats` walks all folders (`folder=None`), hence the difference. The answer had
no field saying anything was left out.
