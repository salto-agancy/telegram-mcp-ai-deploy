# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on the technical problem.
- Respect privacy: never post another person's credentials, infrastructure or Telegram data.
- Prefer the smallest safe contribution and document uncertainty honestly.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information,
and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions and other spaces where someone
represents the project. Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and may ban
participants for behavior they consider inappropriate or unsafe.

Report conduct incidents privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/salto-agancy/telegram-mcp-ai-deploy/security/advisories/new)
when public discussion would expose a person or sensitive information. Maintainers will
respect the reporter's privacy as far as practical.
